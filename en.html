Qiuhua Xu

Ph.D. in Economics · School of Finance, SWUFE

I am an Associate Professor and Deputy Chair of the Department of Financial Technology at the School of Finance, Southwestern University of Finance and Economics (SWUFE). My research interests include financial econometrics, applied microeconometrics, asset pricing, statistical learning, and financial technology.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics at Xiamen University. I was a visiting doctoral researcher at the University of Kansas and Humboldt University of Berlin. I welcome inquiries from students and colleagues interested in related topics.
